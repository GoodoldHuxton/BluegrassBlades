title=Bluegrass Blades [B42]
description=[h1]Bluegrass Blades [B42][/h1]
description=
description=Knives for Kentucky, 1993. This mod adds [b]38 knife variants[/b] in five families, plus three forgeable blades. They use the vanilla models with new textures, and their stats sit close to the vanilla knives they are based on.
description=
description=[h2]What's in it[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Bowie Knife[/b] (based on the Hunting Knife)
description=[*][b]Fillet Knife[/b]
description=[*][b]Lockback Folding Knife[/b]
description=[*][b]Trapper Pocketknife[/b] (slip-joint)
description=[*][b]Balisong[/b] (uses the vanilla butterfly knife model)
description=[/list]
description=Planned: a Kukri with its own model, for a future update.
description=
description=[h2]How the variants work[/h2]
description=Each knife has three parts:
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Grade[/b] sets the stats: Military, Sporting, Handmade, Knockoff (best to worst). Knockoffs are cheap and wear out quickly.
description=[*][b]Handle[/b] changes looks and rarity only: Walnut, Bone, Leather, Polymer, Ivory.
description=[*][b]Finish[/b] is looks and rarity only: Satin, Blued, Black Oxide, Rusted, Temper Color, Damascus.
description=[/list]
description=Ivory handles are rare and turn up in gun, camping, antique and pawn shops. Temper Color is rare and Damascus is very rare.
description=
description=[h2]Loot and crafting[/h2]
description=Knives spawn where vanilla knives do: army surplus, gun and camping stores, hunting and fishing spots, pawn shops, antiques, garages and junk crates. Military grade comes from army loot, Knockoffs from cheap and junk loot.
description=
description=With Blacksmithing you can forge Fillet, Bowie and Damascus Bowie blades and assemble them into Handmade knives. Researching a looted knife of the same type can teach you its forge recipe. Damascus needs Blacksmithing 8.
description=
description=[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]Built for Project Zomboid [b]42.21[/b]. Other versions are not tested.
description=[*][b]Single player tested.[/b] Coop and multiplayer have not been tested yet.
description=[*]New items only. No vanilla items are changed or removed.
description=[/list]
